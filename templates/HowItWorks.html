How JetMyProjects Works

Your complete guide to mastering deadline management and project tracking. Follow these simple steps to stay organized and never miss a deadline again.

1

Create Your Account

Getting started is easy! Simply click the "Sign Up" button and create your free account in seconds. All you need is an email address and a secure password. We'll send you a verification code to ensure your account is secure.

  • Quick registration process
  • Email verification for security
  • Choose from multiple pricing plans
  • Start with our free tier
2

Create Your First Project

Once logged in, you'll land on your intuitive dashboard. Click "Create New Project" to get started. Add project details including title, description, deadline, and priority level. You can also attach files and documents to keep everything organized in one place.

  • Add unlimited projects (plan dependent)
  • Set clear deadlines and priorities
  • Attach files and documents
  • Categorize with custom tags
3

Track Your Progress

Monitor all your projects from a single dashboard. View active projects, upcoming deadlines, completed tasks, and drafts. Our smart deadline tracking system highlights critical tasks and helps you prioritize what matters most.

  • Real-time dashboard overview
  • Color-coded urgency indicators
  • Filter by status (Active, Draft, Completed)
  • Visual progress tracking
4

Edit & Update Anytime

Life changes, and so do deadlines. Easily edit project details, extend deadlines, mark tasks as complete, or move projects to drafts. Our flexible system adapts to your workflow, not the other way around.

  • Quick edit functionality
  • Mark projects as complete or incomplete
  • Move to drafts for future reference
  • Delete unwanted projects
5

Stay Organized & On Track

Never miss a deadline again! JetMyProjects keeps you organized with automatic sorting, deadline reminders, and storage management. View your completed projects history and manage your account storage efficiently.

  • Automatic deadline sorting
  • Storage usage monitoring
  • Project completion history
  • Easy file management
