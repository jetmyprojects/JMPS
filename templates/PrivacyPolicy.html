1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you use our Service. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. We serve users 13 years and older and do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: First Name, Last Name, Affiliation, Organization, email address, and password (hashed).
  • Subscription & Billing: Payment details processed by Stripe; we do not store full credit card numbers.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages viewed, timestamps.
  • Cookies: Used with consent for analytics and functionality.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and secure the Service.
  • Manage subscriptions and payments (via Stripe).
  • Communicate service updates and authentication codes (via Zoho ZeptoMail email).
  • Analyze usage for performance (via Google Analytics).
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Cookies & Analytics

  • We use Google Analytics for product performance only (no advertising features).
  • A granular cookie banner allows you to manage consent for analytics cookies.

5. Payment Processing

Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not store payment card details. Stripe processes data under its own privacy policy.

Refunds: If you cancel your subscription before the end of your annual term, we will issue a pro-rated refund for the unused portion of your subscription on a monthly basis. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

6. Data Sharing

We share data only with trusted service providers:

  • Hosting: DigitalOcean (Virginia, USA)
  • Payments: Stripe
  • Email: Zoho ZeptoMail
  • Analytics: Google Analytics

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active.
  • Deletion: When you delete your account, we purge data within 30 days.
  • Backups & Logs: Retained for 90 days, then deleted or overwritten.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data.
  • Opt out of marketing (if applicable).
  • Manage cookie preferences.

For requests, email privacy@jetmyprojects.com.

9. Security

We use encryption (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, access controls, and monitoring to protect data. No system is 100% secure; we will notify you of breaches as required by law.

10. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be communicated via email or the Service.
